## Attribution and publication decision

The board serves KCB roles across multiple countries, and the observed vacancy
records did not provide a per-role legal-employer field. Both jobs are therefore
labeled **KCB Group** and are not assigned the narrower `KCB Bank Kenya`
employer ID. This preserves country eligibility while avoiding a false
employer-to-subsidiary attribution. The original application links and source
publisher attribution are retained.

Both jobs are distinct from accepted vacancies, have no synthetic posting
dates, and meet the existing technical and African-eligibility rules. The
isolated trial and publication review passed. The source is activated with
absence-based expiry disabled.
